Silver Meridian Classifieds — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Silver Meridian (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the Silver Meridian Classifieds mobile app and related services. We process personal data in accordance with applicable data protection law, including the UK GDPR and, where applicable, the EU GDPR.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to users of Silver Meridian Classifieds. The service is intended only for adults aged 18 or over.

2. Data we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect or process:

  • Account and contact data: account identifiers and email address, together with any profile name or other contact information you choose to provide.
  • Profile and classified content: advert text, profile information, preferences, categories, photos and other content you submit.
  • Messages and user communications: messages sent through the app and information needed to operate conversations, blocking and reporting features.
  • Location data: approximate or coarse location used to support relevant discovery and location-based app features. We do not intend to require continuous precise GPS tracking for ordinary use.
  • Purchase and entitlement data: App Store transaction identifiers, receipts or entitlement records needed to validate purchases and provide paid features. We do not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.
  • Usage and interaction data: app interactions such as advert views, flips, saves, shares and other feature usage used to operate performance analytics and improve the service.
  • Safety and moderation data: reports, blocks, moderation outcomes, audit information and related records used to detect abuse, scams, prohibited content and safety risks.
  • Advertising data: information necessary to display and measure banner advertising. Depending on the advertising configuration, an advertising provider may process device-level identifiers or information about ads shown.
  • Technical and diagnostic data: device/app information, IP-derived information, crash, performance or security data where collected by our infrastructure or service providers.
  • Sensitive information you choose to provide: because personal adverts may concern identity, relationships and dating preferences, content you voluntarily submit may reveal information treated as sensitive under data protection law. Please only provide information you are comfortable sharing.

3. How we use personal data

  • to create and maintain accounts and authenticate users;
  • to create, publish, display, renew and manage classified adverts and profiles;
  • to provide messaging, blocking, reporting, saved-ad and performance features;
  • to validate purchases, subscriptions, rewards and entitlements;
  • to moderate content and protect users from fraud, scams, harassment and other abuse;
  • to operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the app;
  • to display and measure banner advertising where enabled;
  • to comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests;
  • to communicate service, safety, account or policy information.

4. PAM, PAM-CAM and AI-assisted features

Silver Meridian may offer AI-assisted editorial, moderation or image-related features, including PAM and PAM-CAM. Where you choose to use these features, relevant text, images or contextual information may be processed by automated systems or specialist service providers for the requested feature, moderation, safety or quality purposes.

We aim to minimise the data sent for AI processing and use it only for the relevant product function, safety requirement or service improvement permitted by law. We do not sell your personal data to AI providers.

5. Legal bases

Depending on the processing activity, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • performance of our contract with you, where processing is necessary to provide the service you request;
  • our legitimate interests in operating, securing, moderating and improving Silver Meridian Classifieds;
  • your consent, where consent is required or appropriate;
  • compliance with legal obligations.

Where special-category information is voluntarily provided in user content, we will process it only where a lawful condition permits us to do so.

6. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with service providers that help us operate Silver Meridian Classifieds, for example:

  • Apple, for App Store distribution, StoreKit purchases and related account/platform services;
  • cloud hosting, authentication, database, storage, analytics and security providers, including Firebase/Google services where used;
  • advertising technology providers used to serve banner advertising;
  • AI or automated-processing providers used for optional editorial, image, moderation or safety features;
  • professional advisers, regulators or law-enforcement bodies where disclosure is legally required or necessary to protect safety and rights.

Service providers are expected to process data only for the relevant service and under appropriate contractual and security controls.

7. Advertising and tracking

The app may display banner advertising. Advertising providers may receive information required to deliver, limit, secure or measure ads. Whether data is used for personalised advertising or tracking depends on the active advertising configuration and the permissions you provide.

Where Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework applies, we will request permission before accessing data or identifiers for tracking as defined by Apple. You can also control relevant permissions in your device settings.

8. International data transfers

Some service providers may process data outside the UK or European Economic Area. Where required, we use recognised safeguards such as adequacy decisions, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including providing the service, maintaining security and moderation records, resolving disputes, preventing abuse and meeting legal obligations.

Different categories of data may have different retention periods. Some safety, purchase, audit or fraud-prevention records may need to be retained after an advert expires or an account is closed where there is a lawful reason to do so.

10. Account deletion

Silver Meridian Classifieds provides an in-app account deletion process. When you request deletion, we will delete or de-identify personal data that we no longer need, subject to legitimate retention requirements such as security, fraud prevention, moderation evidence, purchase records or legal obligations.

11. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including access controls, authenticated services, encryption in transit, rules-based data access and moderation/security controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to request portability, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or another competent data protection authority.

13. Children

Silver Meridian Classifieds is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly permit children to use the service. If we become aware that an under-18 user has created an account, we may restrict or remove the account and associated content as appropriate.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, our providers, our advertising or AI configuration, or applicable law changes. We will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
